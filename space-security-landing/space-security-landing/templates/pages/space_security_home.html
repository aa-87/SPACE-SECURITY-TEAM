01 / FIRST CONTACT

INITIAL OBJECTIVE — NICOTINE

Break the loop.
Keep the data.

The first field of study is quitting cigarettes. Not through shame or slogans, but by making the invisible visible: triggers, cravings, choices, setbacks, and the small wins that add up.

The planned logbook will help you record moments as they happen and see your own patterns over time. Your progress is personal. The point is to learn what helps you move forward.

Your story.
Your control.

Personal growth should not require handing over your identity.

The intended product direction is pseudonymous and data-minimal: no real-name profile, no public activity feed, and no sale of personal progress data. Privacy and security will be treated as design requirements, not marketing decoration.

MISSION STATEMENT / REV 01PUBLIC

I want to document my self-directed discoveries and experiments across different areas of life, beginning with the challenge of quitting cigarettes.

The larger idea is to build a “Space Security Team” inspired by the scale and wonder of the Milky Way: a community of people working to strengthen their physical capabilities, mental discipline, and capacity to learn.

The first web experience will be a private, self-guided logbook for understanding nicotine habits and visualizing progress. Over time, the project may expand into other personal-development topics, carefully documented experiments, and an introduction to MUMPS programming as a form of intellectual training.

The application is intended to be privacy-first and pseudonymous. The project aims to operate for community benefit, with an open and transparent development process. It is not currently represented as a registered nonprofit, medical service, or HIPAA-certified platform.

Personal stories will be labeled as personal stories. Research will be distinguished from opinion. Uncertainty, risks, and failed experiments will not be hidden to make the project look more convincing.
