MISSION STATEMENT / REV 01PUBLIC

I want to document my self-directed discoveries and experiments across different areas of life, beginning with the challenge of quitting cigarettes.

The larger idea is to build a “Space Security Team” inspired by the scale and wonder of the Milky Way: a community of people working to strengthen their physical capabilities, mental discipline, and capacity to learn.

The web experience includes a private, self-guided cessation log and a separate breath-observation log. A unified dashboard brings their local records together. Over time, the project may expand into other personal-development topics, carefully documented experiments, and an introduction to MUMPS programming as a form of intellectual training.

The application is intended to be privacy-first and pseudonymous. The project aims to operate for community benefit, with an open and transparent development process. It is not currently represented as a registered nonprofit, medical service, or HIPAA-certified platform.

Personal stories will be labeled as personal stories. Research will be distinguished from opinion. Uncertainty, risks, and failed experiments will not be hidden to make the project look more convincing.

02 / FIELD LOG 002

AVAILABLE NOW / OFFLINE / LOCAL ONLY

Record the breath.
Keep the context.

A standalone observation log for recording individual breath-hold durations, dates, and optional context. Durations are entered after the fact; this is not a live hold timer, training plan, or fitness test.

The founder reports finding breath-hold practice personally useful during recovery from a leg injury, when typical cardio activity was difficult. That experience is preserved as personal history, not proof of improved cardiovascular fitness or a substitute for exercise.
