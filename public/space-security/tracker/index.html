Data controls

Export creates a copy you control. Import replaces the current local record after validation. Keep exported files somewhere private.

Privacy, plainly stated. This version stores entries in this browser's local storage. There is no account, server sync, analytics, or transmission to Space Security Team. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access its data. Use a private device profile and export/delete data thoughtfully.
Health context. Your personal experience matters and deserves careful documentation. It does not establish that combining cannabis and tobacco is a proven or low-risk cessation treatment. Both smoked substances can expose you to harmful combustion products; tobacco maintains nicotine dependence, and cannabis has its own risks. This tracker records your choices without endorsing smoking or suggesting a ratio. Consider discussing cessation options with a qualified clinician.
