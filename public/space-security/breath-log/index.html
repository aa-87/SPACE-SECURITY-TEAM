A private, offline-first log for recording individual breath-hold durations and their context. This is record-keeping — not a training plan, fitness test, or instruction to hold your breath longer.

SAFETY FIRST — PLEASE READ

Breath-holding can cause dizziness, fainting, blackout, or drowning. Do not use this log to train, test limits, or compete. Never practice in or near water, while driving, standing, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could injure you. Do not hyperventilate or use rapid/deep breathing to prepare. Do not practice alone. Stop if you feel unwell. This app provides no medical or fitness guidance; consult a qualified clinician about whether breath-hold activity is appropriate for you.
