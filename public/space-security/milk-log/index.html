The founding hypothesis

The founder reports a personal impression that calcium intake may be associated with better milk tolerance. This is unverified: lactose intolerance results from difficulty digesting lactose due to low lactase activity. Calcium does not replace lactase and is not a proven treatment. Do not increase calcium doses or deliberately consume foods that cause symptoms to test this idea. Milk allergy is different; avoid known allergens. Seek medical advice for persistent symptoms, and emergency care for breathing difficulty or throat swelling.
